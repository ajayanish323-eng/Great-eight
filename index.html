Welcome Days — Say Hello to October

🎯 Target: A gentle first look at the new month and our new autumn topic.

🍂
Day 1 & Day 2Introduction — October 1 & 2
🗣️ English
Children will be introduced to the autumn season and discover how the world around them changes after summer. Through simple conversations, pictures, movement, creative activities and sensory exploration, they will notice changes in weather, nature and clothing and become familiar with the new season.
